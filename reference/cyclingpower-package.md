# cyclingpower: Compare readings from multiple cycling power meters

Read FIT, GPX and CSV ride files recorded by two or more power meters,
align them onto a common clock, and quantify how their readings differ
overall and across the power range. Supports linking a third meter to
the first two through a bridge meter common to both rides. The bundled
app can also record the meters itself over Web Bluetooth, so every
source is stamped by one clock.
